GlowFollow Support

GlowFollow helps parents record how their child looks at faces, gaze cues and movement. The app interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

Getting started

Read and accept the privacy notice, create a child profile, and select Start Assessment. Allow camera access when prompted. Follow the preparation and calibration instructions, keeping the child's face visible and the phone steady in good, even lighting.

Camera access

If camera access was denied, open the iPhone's Settings, find GlowFollow, and enable Camera. Return to GlowFollow and start a new assessment. Eye tracking requires a physical iPhone with a working front camera.

Calibration or tracking problems

Check that the front camera is unobstructed and the child's face is visible. Reduce glare, avoid backlighting, and keep the phone still. Pause and try again if the child is tired or uncomfortable. Missing data or an unavailable result is not a finding about the child's development.

Results and previous records

Open the child's profile to view saved records and their trend. Select two records to compare the available measurements. Results are descriptive, can vary between sessions, and are not a medical diagnosis or an ability or risk assessment.

Delete data

You can delete a child profile and its records, or delete local data from Settings. There is no online account to close. Deleting the app removes its local data; device and computer backups are managed separately.

Change language

In Settings > Language, choose English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese or System Default. Reopen the app for system permission prompts to follow the selected language.

Privacy

Camera frames are processed on the device and are not saved or uploaded. This public version has no research invitation or research data sharing. Read the privacy policy for the data stored locally and your options for deleting it.
