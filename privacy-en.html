GlowFollow Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 10, 2026

Introduction

GlowFollow ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is used by a parent or guardian: the child watches a few short animations, the front camera estimates on the device where the child looks, and the App then shows descriptive results such as focus. The App is intended for children aged 12 to 72 months.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is used and stored, and what rights you have. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information the App Handles

1.1 Child Profile

  • Child's first name or nickname
  • Date of birth
  • Gender ("Other" may be selected)

1.2 Gaze Data

  • Estimated gaze coordinates and their timestamps during an assessment
  • Tracking states such as face missing, eyes out of bounds, calibration required, and dropped frame
  • Recording quality metrics such as effective sample rate and dropped-frame rate
  • Technical information needed to interpret the data: tracking engine and algorithm versions, calibration plan, screen size, device model, and iOS version

1.3 Assessment Results

  • Assessment date and time, and which animations were shown
  • Descriptive results such as focus, faces vs. shapes, following gaze, and walking figure vs. scrambled dots, plus gaze timelines

1.4 Settings on This Device

  • Whether and when you accepted this Privacy Policy
  • A randomly generated identifier used only to separate your data on this device; the App does not require an account

2. How the Information Is Used

  • Running assessments: recording where your child looks during the animations and checking that the recording is complete.
  • Showing results: calculating and displaying descriptive results. No ability score, risk label, or diagnostic conclusion is generated.
  • Reviewing history: keeping records so you can view trends and compare two records.

We do not use any data for advertising, marketing, or purposes unrelated to these functions.

3. Storage and Security

  • All of the information above is stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework and system settings, inside the App sandbox and protected by iOS device security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID).
  • The App does not upload any data to our servers or to third parties, and does not sync data with iCloud.
  • Depending on your device settings, iOS or computer backups may include App data. The App does not manage those backups; deleting data in the App does not delete existing backups.
  • The App contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

4. Camera and On-Device Processing

  • The App uses the front camera and the on-device GazeFollower eye-tracking engine to estimate gaze.
  • Camera frames are processed in memory only. The App does not save or upload any photos, videos, or camera frames.
  • The App does not create facial-recognition templates or biometric identifiers.
  • Only the gaze results, tracking states, quality metrics, and technical information listed in Section 1.2 are saved.

5. Children's Privacy

  • The App is operated by a parent or guardian on behalf of the child. Children do not enter any information themselves.
  • We handle only the minimum information needed to run an assessment, and all of it stays on the device.
  • We do not show advertising to children and do not use children's data for commercial purposes.

6. Your Rights

  • Access: you can view your child's profile and all assessment records in the App at any time.
  • Correction: you can edit your child's name, date of birth, and gender at any time.
  • Deletion: you can delete a single child's profile and records, or delete all local data in Settings. Deleting the App removes its local data from this device; existing device or computer backups are managed separately.

7. Data Retention

Data remains on your device until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

8. Medical Disclaimer

GlowFollow is NOT a medical device and is NOT intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition.

  • Results are for reference only. They are not a medical diagnosis and do not assess ability or risk.
  • Results are affected by your child's mood, lighting, and posture. Look at the overall pattern across several records.
  • If you have concerns about your child's development, consult a pediatrician or a developmental specialist. Do not make medical decisions based solely on the App's results.

9. Changes to This Policy

When this policy is updated, we will publish the new version in the App and update the "Last Updated" date above. Continuing to use the App after a change is published means you accept the updated policy.
